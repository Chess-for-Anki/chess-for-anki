# Optional voice packs

Installing Chess for Anki does not download recordings. System speech is the default. Natural recordings can be installed separately, keeping the core add-on small.

| Pack | Coverage | Download |
| --- | --- | --- |
| English Michael - Free Sample 1.6 | Explanations for all 22 free-sample cards, move announcements, titles, levels and hints used by those lessons. Paired sides share recordings. | [ZIP](https://github.com/Chess-for-Anki/chess-for-anki/releases/download/v1.6.0-preview/English-Michael-Free-Sample-1.6.zip) |
| Earlier English Michael | Earlier Starter and Foundations material. | [ZIP](https://github.com/Chess-for-Anki/chess-for-anki/releases/download/v1.4.2-preview/English-Michael-Voice-Pack.zip) |

1. Install the current add-on preview from the [GitHub release](https://github.com/Chess-for-Anki/chess-for-anki/releases/tag/v1.6.0-preview).
2. Download the pack matching your lessons.
3. Open **Chess for Anki > Language packs** and import the ZIP without extracting it.
4. Choose its commentary and narrator in the manager. A profile selects one narrator pack at a time; recordings for missing phrases can fall back to system speech.
5. Adjust playback speed in Settings or on the card. The new sample recordings are stored at their original speed, so changing speed does not modify the files.

Audio and text remain separate downloads from the deck. Install the pack on each desktop where you want to use it. Media can sync through AnkiWeb; desktop add-on settings and installation are separate.

Only English natural recordings are currently provided. System voices for other languages depend on the device. Additional natural languages and Hindi runtime support remain planned. [Contribute a language or recording](CONTRIBUTING-LANGUAGES.md).

Michael is a synthetic voice from the licensed local Kokoro model, not an imitation of a streamer or a recording of the developer. See the pack metadata and [credits](CREDITS.md). This pack contains no paid training lessons.
