# Training collections

## Free sample

The free sample contains 22 cards: five beginner opening lessons, each from
both sides, and three exercises each for Brilliancy, endgames, middlegames
and short tactics. The openings follow a complete selected training line
into a clear middlegame position. They are not exhaustive repertoires.

Every exercise that begins midgame includes the preceding opponent move,
with its origin and destination highlighted. Composed exercises use a
labelled, legally validated setup move. Brilliant-move effects and sounds
can be disabled separately in Settings.

[Download the free sample](https://github.com/Chess-for-Anki/chess-for-anki/releases/download/v1.6.0-preview/Chess-for-Anki-Free-Sample.apkg)

## Extended packs and PRO

The extended catalogue is in preparation. Planned packs cover gambits and
unusual opening branches, calculation, middlegame decisions, endgames and
historical brilliant combinations. Individual pack purchases and monthly
or annual access are planned. There is no active paid checkout yet.

The add-on remains usable with your own PGN and FEN lessons. Buying a pack
will provide curated lessons, commentary and updates. A future cancellation
will end new downloads and updates after the paid period; it will not erase
cards or review history already saved in your local Anki collection.

No AI service integration is planned. Additional language recordings remain
separate downloads when produced and reviewed. Available voices and pack
contents will be listed explicitly before any purchase.

Support links are voluntary contributions, not purchases or automatic PRO
activation. Existing downloads remain available to their previous users;
previously downloaded files are not remotely revoked.
