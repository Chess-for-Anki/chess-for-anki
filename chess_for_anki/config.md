Use **Chess for Anki → Settings** to select the next training level, enable the first-view demonstration, and control piece sounds.

The card has **Voice on/off**, **Voice speed** and **Demo on first view** controls. Narration defaults to 1.5× and can be changed from 0.5× to 2×. **Language packs** selects the basic system language or an optional natural voice ZIP. The add-on does not create cards or install voice recordings automatically.

**Brilliancy Training** has separate settings for the turquoise highlight and `!!` badge, celebration particles, and sound. These activate after a correct, curated key move; they never identify the solution before the attempt. Particles respect your device's reduced-motion preference. The brilliant sound also follows **Piece sounds**. Choose the included original chime or import your own MP3/WAV (up to 2 MB); imported audio stays in `user_files` and is embedded into your card templates when saved.
