# Commentary references

The move explanations were written for this repertoire. Opening principles were checked against the user's supplied books, especially Carsten Hansen's Back to Basics: Openings, chapter 2, and Irving Chernev's Logical Chess: Move by Move, game 1. Gary Lane's The Scotch Game Explained provides further reading on the Scotch structures.

The books are reading references. Their text, scans and diagrams are excluded from this distribution.

Opening move sequences come from lichess-org/chess-openings, under CC0. The historical game records and source links appear in the starter cards' Book Notes. The commentary describes both sides' played intentions, including material grabs and defensive choices that allow the later combinations.

The same position can support multiple playable moves. Each card trains the saved continuation. No engine evaluation or claim of uniquely optimal play is attached to the explanations.
